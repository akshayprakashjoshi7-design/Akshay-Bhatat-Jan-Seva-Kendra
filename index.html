Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an official government website?

No. This is an independent directory linking to external websites. Always verify the destination URL before entering personal information.

Can I use the portal without an account?

Yes. All public directory links, calendar navigation and search work in Guest Mode.

Can AKKI AI answer any question?

No. It only helps locate resources and features within this website.

Why do some links open other websites?

The portal is a directory. Services and account transactions take place on the provider's own site.

Are the holiday dates official?

The calendar shows dates, not verified holiday declarations. Refer to the appropriate official state or central notification.
